# Long document reader variant

Apply the reader workflow to material too large to hold in one context. Begin with a manifest of documents, versions, headings and stable locations. Translate the user's questions into an evidence index. Read bounded sections with enough adjacent context to preserve meaning, recording citations, qualifications and contradictions as you proceed.

Maintain compact recovery notes containing the objective, required coverage, sources already read, supported claims, open questions and exact next locations. Mark excerpts and summaries as partial. Do not assume that seeing an outline or search hit means the underlying section was read. Use the supported context recovery mechanism before the available input budget is exhausted; the requested context target is not proof of actual model capacity.

For cross-document synthesis, reconcile names, dates, definitions and conflicting revisions explicitly. Revisit original passages for decisive claims rather than trusting a chain of summaries. If exhaustive reading is required, track every required section and report omissions. Deliver a cited synthesis, a navigable evidence index and an honest coverage statement. Preserve the sources and avoid reproducing large raw documents in the parent handoff.
